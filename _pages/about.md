My name is Marcos Fernández-Pichel. I received a Ph.D. in Computer Science in 2023 from the University of Santiago de Compostela and Universidade da Coruña, graduating *cum laude*. I am currently an Assistant Professor at the Electronics and Computer Science Department from the University of Santiago de Compostela. I am also a research collaborator from the Centro Singular de Investigación en Tecnoloxías Intelixentes ([CiTIUS](https://citius.gal/es/)). The Royal Galician Academy of Sciences awarded me with the best paper conducted by a young researcher in 2022. My current research interests are Information Retrieval (IR) and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

I have published my research in top-notch conferences—such as SIGIR (CORE A*), EMNLP (CORE A*), ECIR (CORE A)—and high-impact journals, including IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing (D1) and NPJ Digital Medicine (D1).

Beyond research, I am actively involved in the academic community: I served as a main organizer for the 6th edition of the ROMCIR workshop (Reducing Online Misinformation through Credible Information Retrieval) and was a member of the local organizing committee for ECAI 2024. I am also a frequent Program Committee member for conferences like ECIR and CIKM, and a regular reviewer for journals such as Scientific Reports and The Journal of Supercomputing.
